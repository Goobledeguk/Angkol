1.Introduction
1.1 Purpose
The Angkol platform aims to modernize local commuting by delivering point-to-point ride-hailing for tricycles in limited-access areas (e.g., alleys, narrow streets) and real-time tracking, scheduling, and route mapping for Public Utility Jeepneys (PUJs) and Public Utility Vehicles (PUVs). It ensures transparent, fixed fares to minimize disputes and optimizes vehicle capacity for drivers. By connecting commuters and local transportation providers through a centralized digital platform, Angkol intends to provide a more predictable transportation experience while helping drivers identify nearby passengers and improve the efficiency of their trips.
1.2 Intended Audience
This platform is set for the following user, where they utilize the whole application: 

Users 

Description and Responsibilities



Commuter / Passenger

The primary user of Angkol who can request point-to-point tricycle rides, view fixed fares before booking, locate nearby transportation terminals, monitor live PUJ/PUV locations, and identify available vehicles along designated routes.


Tricycle Driver

Uses the mobile application to receive and manage ride requests, view passenger pickup and destination details, accept or reject bookings, update ride status, and check the fare associated with a trip.


PUJ/PUV Driver

Uses the application to share vehicle location and route information, monitor passenger requests along the designated route, and identify potential passenger pickup points without unnecessary distractions while driving.


Administrators

Uses the web-based management platform to monitor trips, manage user accounts and transportation information, investigate reported issues, review system logs, analyze transportation data, and identify areas for system maintenance and improvement.




1.3 Product Scope
Angkol will provide a digital platform where commuters can request tricycle rides, view available transportation options, obtain estimated and fixed fare information, locate nearby PUJ/PUV terminals, view PUJ/PUV routes, and access available transportation schedules and tracking information. Registered tricycle drivers will be able to receive ride requests, view passenger pickup locations, manage their availability, and accept or decline requests. The system will also provide administrative functions for managing driver accounts, transportation routes, fare information, terminals, reports, and system records.
The initial scope will focus on local transportation services and information within the selected implementation area. The system will not initially function as a full public transportation payment processor, vehicle dispatching authority, courier service or government transportation management system. Online payments, automated government enforcement, advanced demand prediction, and integration with every transportation operator may remain outside the initial implementation unless they are specifically needed in later development phases.

2. Overall Description
2.1 Product Perspective
Angkol is a centralized transportation platform consisting of mobile applications for commuters and drivers and a web-based platform for administrators and developers. It uses smartphone GPS, internet connectivity, a backend server, and a relational database to support ride requests, fixed-fare information, vehicle tracking, and transportation records. The system may integrate with third-party mapping and notification services, while its administration platform supports trip monitoring, system management, and analytics. The following table summarizes its major components and external interactions:

2.2 Product Functions
The system functions as follow: 
Tricycle ride-hailing for point-to-point requests from narrow streets with automatic fixed-fare calculation. 
PUJ/PUV tracking that shows real-time vehicle locations, terminal locations, and estimated departure schedules.
Fare transparency that displays LGU-based fixed prices upfront to prevent fare dispute.
Commuter should be able to review and choose their available transportation options.
Commuter should be able to see all terminal locations of the transportation vehicles.

2.4 Operating Environment
Angkol is intended to operate primarily on smartphones and computers with an active internet connection. Passenger and driver interfaces may be implemented as responsive web and mobile applications so that the system can support mobile devices and modern desktop browsers. The backend may operate on a web server using a suitable server-side programming framework and relational database.


For the application to work it will require access to GPS or location services for functions involving vehicle location and transportation mapping. A stable internet connection is required for real-time ride requests, communication, and location updates.
2.5 Design and Implementation Constraints
The system shall use a framework like React-Native for mobile responsiveness of the application and for database will use a relational database for structured transportation records  to implement role-based access control to prevent passengers and drivers from accessing unauthorized functions such like PostgreSQL. The design of the system shall be divided into 2 separate platform for better monitoring and control. 
The platform also considers limitations in mobile hardware, internet connectivity, GPS accuracy, server resources, and transportation data availability. Because Angkol handles location and personal information, its implementation must follow applicable data protection and privacy requirements.
2.6 Assumptions and Dependencies
It is assumed that users have smartphones with GPS enabled and mobile data, and that LGU or on the Terminal will provide accurate fare matrix and PUJ/PUV schedules. The system depends on third-party services like APIs and Cloud Messaging. It also assumes drivers will keep location services active and grant location permissions for tracking to work. Any interruption in these external services may reduce the accuracy or availability of transportation information.

3. External Interface Requirements
3.1 User Interfaces (UI) 
Interface
Proposed Features and Description
Commuter Interface
A welcoming and easy-to-navigate mobile interface that supports point-to-point tricycle requests, particularly for narrow streets and alleys. It should display fixed fares before booking, show pickup and destination information, provide ride-status updates, and present available PUJ/PUV locations, terminals, routes, and estimated departure schedules.
Tricycle Driver Interface
A simple mobile layout for viewing incoming ride requests, passenger pickup and destination details, fare information, and booking status. It should provide clear controls for accepting or rejecting requests, managing availability, and updating ride progress.





PUJ/PUV Driver Interface

A driver-oriented interface that supports route and location sharing and presents passenger pickup points along the driver's designated route. Important information should be easy to understand, with minimal interaction required while driving.




Administrator Interface

A web-based dashboard for viewing current and past trips, monitoring reported transportation activity, managing user and transportation records, and reviewing system status. Authorized administrators should also be able to handle reports and support requests.

3.2 Hardware Interfaces
Smartphones and tablets that commuters, drivers, and dispatchers carry in their pockets, providing the portable screen and touch interface needed to access the platform on the go.
Desktop computers or laptops used for monitoring wider system activity. Location and tracking tools 
Built-in smartphone GPS sensors that continuously track locations, allowing the system to accurately map out where tricycles and jeepneys are in real time. 
Cloud servers operating behind the scenes on the internet to act as the central brain of Angkol. 
Powerful hosting capabilities designed to process multiple heavy tasks simultaneously such as live transit tracking feeds, rapid ride-hailing matches between passengers and drivers, and constant database queries ensuring the platform stays fast and stable without lagging or crashing.
3.3 Software Interfaces
 Angkol shall interact with a mobile application, database, GPS/location services, and map services. The system will store information such as user accounts, driver details, vehicle information, routes, fares, terminals, and ride records. It shall use APIs that will help the geo tagging of the specific location within the locality. 
3.4 Communications Interfaces
Angkol shall use an internet connection to exchange information between commuters, drivers, and the server. The system shall use secure communication protocols to protect user information and ride data. Real-time communication will support ride requests, vehicle tracking, notifications, and fare information.



Authentication information and other sensitive data must be transmitted through encrypted connections. Location updates should be transmitted at an appropriate frequency to balance tracking usefulness, network consumption, battery usage, and system performance.

5. Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs)
The following requirements define the expected performance, security, reliability, and software quality of Angkol. They serve as preliminary development guidelines and may be refined through technical evaluation and testing.
5.1 Performance Requirements
These requirements address the system's ability to process ride requests, update vehicle locations, and manage increasing workloads efficiently.
Requirement
Description
Response Time
Common operations, including fare inquiries and ride requests, should respond within acceptable time limits under expected workloads.
Concurrent Users
The system should support multiple commuters and drivers using its services simultaneously.
Database Performance
Data retrieval and storage should remain efficient as user accounts, bookings, and trip records increase.
Location Updates
Vehicle locations and ride statuses should be updated in a timely manner, subject to network and GPS availability.
5.2 Security Requirements
These requirements protect user accounts, personal information, location data, and transportation transactions against unauthorized access and misuse.
Requirement
Description
Access Control
Role-based permissions should distinguish commuters, drivers, administrators, and developers.
Session Management
User sessions should be managed securely, with appropriate expiration and reauthentication when necessary.
Data Encryption
Sensitive information should be protected through appropriate encryption during transmission and storage.

5.3 Reliability and Availability
These requirements ensure that Angkol remains dependable during transportation operations and handles service interruptions without unnecessarily losing essential information.
Requirement
Description
Availability
The system should remain available during its intended operating hours, with an appropriate availability target established before deployment.
Data Consistency
Ride requests, fare records, and trip statuses should remain consistent when multiple updates occur.
Failure Handling
Temporary network, GPS, or external service failures should be handled gracefully, with clear feedback to users.

5.4 Software Quality Attributes
These requirements guide the development team in maintaining a usable, maintainable, scalable, and adaptable application.
Requirement

Database Requirements
The database shall satisfy the following requirements:
User Data ManagementThe database shall store account information for passengers, tricycle drivers, PUJ/PUV drivers, and administrators while maintaining the appropriate user roles and access privileges.
Driver and Vehicle RecordsThe database shall store driver identification, license information, vehicle information, transportation association information, verification status, and availability status.
Ride and Booking RecordsThe database shall store passenger ride requests, pickup locations, destinations, assigned drivers, fares, timestamps, and trip status.


Route and Terminal RecordsThe database shall store transportation routes, route coordinates, terminal locations, and schedule information to support route and transportation searches.
Fare RecordsThe database shall store configured fare matrices, base fares, distance-based charges, additional charges, and the effective dates of fare information.
Trip and Data Analysis RecordsThe database shall retain completed trip information so that the system can calculate and analyze waiting time, travel time, trip volume, fare information, and peak transportation periods.
Data IntegrityPrimary keys, foreign keys, validation rules, and appropriate constraints shall be implemented to prevent invalid or inconsistent records.
Data Retention and BackupImportant transportation and transaction records shall be stored persistently. Regular database backups shall be implemented to reduce the risk of permanent data loss.
